# 8.
Check that the Levi-Civita connection, given in local coordinates by the formula

$\Gamma_{i j}^k=\frac{1}{2} g^{k \ell}\left(\partial_i g_{j \ell}+\partial_j g_{i \ell}-\partial_{\ell} g_{i j}\right),$

is indeed compatible with $g$.

**Proof**

In a standard basis, metric compatibility reads
$$\begin{align*}
    \frac{\partial}{\partial x^i} \left< \frac{\partial}{\partial x^j},\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k} \right> &= \left< \nabla_{\frac{\partial}{\partial x^i}}\frac{\partial}{\partial x^j},\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k} \right>  + \left<\frac{\partial}{\partial x^j},\nabla_{\frac{\partial}{\partial x^i}}\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k} \right>.
\end{align*}$$
Express the right-hand side in terms of Christoffel symbols and the metric tensor
$$
\begin{align*}
\left< \nabla_{\frac{\partial}{\partial x^i}}\frac{\partial}{\partial x^j},\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k} \right>  + \left<\frac{\partial}{\partial x^j},\nabla_{\frac{\partial}{\partial x^i}}\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k} \right> &= \Gamma_{ij}^l g_{lk} + \Gamma_{ik}^l g_{jl} \\
&= \Gamma_{ij}^k + \Gamma_{ik}^j \\
&= \frac{1}{2}(\partial_i g_{jk} + \partial_j g_{ik} - \partial_k g_{ij}) + \frac{1}{2}(\partial_i g_{kj} + \partial_k g_{ij} - \partial_j g_{ik}) \\
&= \frac{1}{2}(\partial_i g_{jk} + \partial_i g_{kj}) \\
&= \partial_i g_{jk}
\end{align*}
$$
