# 1.
Give an example of a complete, noncompact Riemannian manifold where every point lies on a closed geodesic.

**Proof.**

A flat two-dimensional cylinder, e.g., $S^1 \times \mathbb{R}$ with the product metric, is a complete, noncompact Riemannian manifold. For any point $p=(x,y) \in S^1 \times \mathbb{R}$, the circle $S^1 \times \{y\}$ is a closed geodesic containing $p$.
