# 1.
Give an example of a complete, noncompact Riemannian manifold where every point lies on a closed geodesic.

**Proof.**

A flat two-dimensional cylinder serves as an example of a complete, noncompact Riemannian manifold where every point lies on a closed geodesic, as its core circle is orthogonal to a ray.
