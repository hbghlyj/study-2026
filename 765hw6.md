# 1.
(Koszul's formula for the Levi-Civita connection) Given vector fields $X, Y$, and $Z$, define $K(X, Y, Z):=\frac{1}{2}(X\langle Y, Z\rangle+Y\langle Z, X\rangle-Z\langle X, Y\rangle-\langle Y,[X, Z]\rangle-\langle Z,[Y, X]\rangle+\langle X,[Z, Y]\rangle)$.
(a) Check that $K$ is tensorial in $X$ and $Z$.
(b) Check that it satisfies the following Leibniz rule in $Y$ :

$K(X, f Y, Z)=f K(X, Y, Z)+\langle X(f) Y, Z\rangle .$

(c) Show that we may define a connection $\nabla$ on $T M$ by the prescription

$\left\langle\nabla_X Y, Z\right\rangle=K(X, Y, Z)$

(d) Prove that $\nabla$ is in fact the Levi-Civita connection.
# 2.
The Poincaré ball model of hyperbolic space is the metric on $B_1(0) \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ given by

$g_{i j}=\frac{4 \delta_{i j}}{\left(1-|x|^2\right)^2}$

(a) Calculate the Christoffel symbols of $g_{i j}$.
(b) Show that the intersection of a coordinate axis with $B_1(0)$ is the image of a complete geodesic.
